Seasonality Interpretation

Cyclical month encoding allows our Multiple Linear Regression model to understand seasonal peaks. In time series, standard numbers 1 through 12 can confuse models because December (12) is close to January (1), but standard numerical representations place them at opposite ends. Encoding month as a circular function ($\sin$ and $\cos$ projections) preserves this closeness.

Holt-Winters (Triple Exponential Smoothing) tracks the level, trend, and seasonal components directly. It automatically aligns historical seasonal patterns with the future steps. For daily data, it captures the weekly cyclic pattern (7-day period). For monthly data, it maps the yearly cyclicality (12-month period).
